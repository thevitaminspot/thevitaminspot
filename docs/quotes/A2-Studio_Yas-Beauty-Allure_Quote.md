## 02 — What you will get (full stack)

**Custom website** — Home, shop, services, gallery, about, contact, booking, and checkout. Branded for Yas Beauty Allure, not a generic template.

**Online shop** — Product catalogue, product pages, cart, and checkout. Customers pay online via **Paystack** (NGN). Order confirmation emails and a private order link to track status.

**Fulfillment** — Delivery zones and fees and/or pickup at your location (configured in admin).

**Service booking** — Clients pick a service, date, and time from your available slots. A **booking fee is required** — they must pay online via Paystack before the appointment is confirmed (no “book now, pay later” for services).

**Cancellation policy (on the site)** — **Cancellations are not free.** The booking fee is not treated as a refundable hold; your policy (fee kept, or partial refund only within a window you set) is shown clearly before payment and in booking emails. Clients must agree before they pay the booking fee.

**Client accounts** — Sign up / sign in to see orders and appointments. Guest checkout and guest booking still work.

**Admin dashboard** — Manage products (prices, stock, photos), services, opening hours, blocked days, orders (paid → packed → delivered), appointments, registered clients, and contact messages.

**Payments** — Paystack integration for shop orders and **required service booking fees**; webhook and return-url confirmation so payments stay in sync.

**Email** — Order and booking confirmations from your business domain (once DNS is set up with your developer).

**Google Business Profile** — Set up or claim listing with hours, phone, WhatsApp, photos, and link to **yasbeautyallure.com**.

**Basic SEO** — Titles, descriptions, sitemap, Search Console readiness.

**Handover** — Owner-friendly guide, admin walkthrough, logins, and **30 days** launch support.
